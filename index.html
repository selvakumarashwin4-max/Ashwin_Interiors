About Us

Interior design and execution under one roof

Ashwin Interiors is an interior design and execution company. We handle everything — from the first sketch to the final installation — so you don't have to coordinate designers, carpenters and contractors separately.

We work on an order basis: nothing is mass-produced. Every wardrobe, kitchen, TV unit, panel and ceiling is planned around your room size, your budget and your taste.

  • Custom designs for your exact measurements
  • Design and execution handled by one team
  • Clear communication from quote to handover
